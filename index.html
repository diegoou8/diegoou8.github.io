How I work

Business first

Technology should solve a measurable problem, not exist because a particular technology is interesting.

Build, don't just recommend

I enjoy going beyond analysis and actually creating the application, pipeline, automation, or system required to solve the problem.

Understand the whole system

A database problem can actually be a business-process problem. A reporting problem can actually be a data-model problem. An AI problem can actually be a permissions problem.

Automate repetition

If someone is repeatedly downloading a file, modifying a spreadsheet, running the same query, or manually preparing the same report, I immediately start thinking about how that process can disappear.

Keep learning

Technology evolves quickly. I enjoy working in areas where I constantly have to understand something new, whether that's cloud architecture, authentication, AI protocols, financial reporting logic, or an unfamiliar industry.
